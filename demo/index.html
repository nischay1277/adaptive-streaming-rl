QoE objective: quality − rebuffer cost − quality-change cost. The coefficients follow the logarithmic QoE variant in Pensieve (Mao et al., SIGCOMM 2017, Eq. 6/Table 1); see the workflow guide for the citation and limitations.

How timing is modeled: the trace and actual encoded segment byte sizes determine estimated download time and rebuffering. The local web server itself is not bandwidth-throttled; playback is accelerated for demonstration.

What the agent observes: recent throughput, current buffer, recent downloads, and candidate segment sizes. PPO does not explain its neural-network choice in natural language; the cards expose its action, guard intervention, and measured outcome.
