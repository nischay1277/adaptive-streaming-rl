The page switches between actual H.264 segments encoded from your source video at six bitrate levels. The PPO side has a safety guard selected on held-out FCC validation traces: it caps a choice if its conservative predicted transfer time exceeds the available buffer plus 2 seconds. Both policies use the same trace sample per chunk and the shared QoE model. Local segment requests are not bandwidth-throttled.
